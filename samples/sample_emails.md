## Grounding Enforcements Applied
1. **Locked Copy Retained**: Greeting structure ("Hi {{first_name}},"), sign-off ("Best,\n{{sender_name}}\nStyleSense AI"), and 4-sentence order are strictly preserved.
2. **Capability Integrity**: Every `pain_point_category` rigorously maps to StyleSense AI's four supported capabilities per Appendix A.
3. **Traceability**: Every claim links to an archived industry press release or cited company report.
4. **Programmatic Validation**: Injected claims that cannot be traced to stored lead attributes or contain unsubstantiated superlative patterns are automatically intercepted and rejected with a 422 error by `GroundingService`.
